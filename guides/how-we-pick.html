Guide

How we pick.

One pass, five steps, and a reject log we publish instead of hiding.

  1. Find gym-relevant amazon.com listings by category — tees, shorts, joggers, socks, bras.
  2. Read the Fabric type or materials line on the product page itself, not the title or the ad copy.
  3. Only 100% cotton gets a card. Everything else goes to the reject log below, with the exact composition we saw.
  4. Order each category by the Amazon star rating recorded during that check. Ratings move; the cards say so.
  5. Flag the traps — listings where solids are 100% cotton but heathers are blends carry a “solids only” note.

What we will not do

  • Invent a fibre percentage, a rating, or a price.
  • List a blend because a category looks thin. That is why the bra section holds 1 item.
  • Pretend cotton beats synthetics for every workout.
