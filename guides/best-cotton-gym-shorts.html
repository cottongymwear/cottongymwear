How to buy without getting burned

  • Check Fabric type on the listing, then check it again for your colour — heathers are frequently blends.
  • Cotton jersey shrinks. Between sizes, size up or expect a shorter inseam after the first wash.
  • Pockets and inseam vary between the same brand’s ASINs; read the bullet points, not the photo.
